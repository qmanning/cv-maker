Welcome to CV Maker

Your résumé, laid out as a real page, with your own AI sitting beside it. Tell it what you want and watch the page change. It opens with a made-up person, Mara Quill, so you can try it straight away.

  1. Connect the AI app you already use. One click, no API key.Claude Desktop, the ChatGPT app and Codex, Claude Code, Cursor and others. CV Maker has no account and takes no keys: your AI app talks to it on this computer only.
  2. Ask it, in that app, for what you want.“Tailor my résumé to this job description.” “Tighten it to one page.” “Make the Halcyon bullets lead with results.” It reads the résumé that's open here.
  3. It edits the page, not a chat window.Changes land on the sheet as one step, with Undo right there. The design can't break: your AI rewrites words and sections, never the layout. If an edit spills onto a second page, CV Maker tells your AI so it can tighten up.
  4. Or just type.Click any text to edit it by hand. Hover between sections for +. The arrows and trash beside a section move or remove it.
  5. The résumé is a file, and it's yours.⌘S saves a plain .html file. Export makes a PDF with real text, a Word document, or a PNG.
