Welcome to CV Maker

The page you're looking at is the résumé. There are no forms. It opens with a made-up person, Mara Quill, so you can see how it works.

  1. Click any text and type.Select words and a format bar appears right there: bold, size, color, links, bullets.
  2. Add, move or remove sections.Hover the gap between two blocks and click +. With the caret in a block, the arrows and trash beside it move, duplicate or delete it. Click the picture to change it.
  3. It keeps itself to one page.“Fit to one page” gently scales the sheet as you write; the paper size and zoom are in the toolbar at the top.
  4. Save it as a file. It's yours.⌘S saves a plain .html file wherever you like. Open it here any time. Your AI assistant can edit that file too, and this page updates as it does.
  5. Export when you're ready.The Export button (top right) makes a PDF with real, selectable text, a Word document, or a PNG.
