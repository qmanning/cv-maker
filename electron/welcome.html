Welcome to IcedCoffee

Your résumé, laid out as a real page, with your own AI sitting beside it. Tell it what you want and watch the page change. It opens with a made-up person, Mara Quill, so you can try it straight away.

  1. Connect the AI app you already use. One click, no API key.Claude Desktop, the ChatGPT app and Codex, Claude Code, Cursor and others. IcedCoffee has no account and takes no keys: your AI app talks to it on this computer only.
  2. Ask it, in that app, for what you want.“Tailor my résumé to this job description.” “Tighten it to one page.” “Make the Halcyon bullets lead with results.” It reads the résumé that's open here.
  3. It edits the page, not a chat window.Changes land on the sheet as one step, with Undo right there. The design can't break: your AI rewrites words and sections, never the layout. If an edit spills onto a second page, IcedCoffee tells your AI so it can tighten up.
  4. Or just type.Click any text to edit it by hand. Hover between sections for +. The arrows and trash beside a section move or remove it.
  5. There's a cover letter too.The two buttons at the right end of the bar switch between your résumé and its cover letter. The letter always wears the résumé's header, so you only write the letter. Your AI can work on either one: “Write a cover letter for this job.” Paste it a job ad and ask for the ATS keywords: they appear in a panel that shows which ones you already use, and where.
  6. They are files, and they're yours.⌘S saves a plain .html file for each. Click the document's name in the bar to reopen a recent one, or type a new name to save a copy. Export makes a PDF with real text, a Word document, or a PNG, of the résumé, the letter, or both.
