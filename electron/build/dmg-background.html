Double-click IcedCoffee to begin

Start it from here. It installs itself.

IcedCoffee is free and unsigned, so macOS needs your OK once.

  1. Double-click IcedCoffee, right here in this window.
  2. macOS blocks it once. Click Done, open the shortcut → scroll down, click Open Anyway.
  3. IcedCoffee opens and offers to install itself. Say yes.
