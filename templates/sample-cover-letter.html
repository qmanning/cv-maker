Dear Jordan,

I'm writing about the Staff Product Designer role on Northline's operator tools team. I've spent the last decade designing for the unglamorous half of transit and robotics, the screens dispatchers and warehouse operators live in all day, and that is the work your posting describes.

At Halcyon Transit I cut fare-purchase time in the rider app from 94 seconds to 31, and I rode more than sixty routes with working operators before drawing a single screen for the new dispatch tool. At Tidewater Robotics I designed the fleet dashboard that let one operator supervise 40 robots instead of 12. In both places the design got better because I went and watched the job being done first.

What draws me to Northline is that you publish your on-time numbers and your misses. I'd like to help the people who run that system see a problem a few minutes sooner than they do today, and I'd bring my own research, working prototypes, and a habit of reading the support tickets.

I'd welcome the chance to talk about what the operator tools team is taking on next year.

Sincerely,
