Portland, Oregon

I like problems that don't have an obvious screen at the end of them. Give me a bus network, a warehouse full of robots, or a support queue nobody wants to own, and I'll go find out how it actually works before I open Figma. I've spent the last decade doing that at Halcyon Transit, Tidewater Robotics, and a small Portland agency called Paperboat Studio, and the thing I'm proudest of is never being the designer who shipped the clean mockup that broke on day one.

These days I lead design for Halcyon's rider app and the operator tools that keep it running, which means I split my time between people riding the bus and people driving it. I write code when it gets an idea in front of engineering faster, I run my own research, and I'd rather ship something rough and true than something polished and wrong. If you want a designer who reads the support tickets, that's me.
